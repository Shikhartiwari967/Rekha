# Code of Conduct

## Our promise

Everyone working on Rekha — team members, reviewers, mentors and judges — gets a respectful,
harassment-free experience, whatever their background, experience level or identity.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are learning.
- Critique code and ideas, never people.
- Ask before assuming; explain your reasoning.
- Give credit for others' work.
- Be honest about what works, what doesn't, and what was not tested.

## Not acceptable

- Insults, harassment, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Publishing anyone's private information.
- Using the attack tooling in this project against systems you are not authorised to test.

## Reporting

Contact a team maintainer (Abhigyan Khare or Shikhar Tiwari). Reports are handled privately
and fairly.
